FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to join?

Not at all! Our Foundation program is designed specifically for beginners. Our coaches will guide you from your very first session.

Can I switch programs after signing up?

Yes, absolutely. Members on the Forge and Elite plans can switch between programs at any time through their member dashboard.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 7-day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

What equipment do I need?

For in-gym programs, all equipment is provided. For home programs, a minimal setup (resistance bands, dumbbells) is recommended.
